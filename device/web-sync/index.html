Send plain text or a web URL to your OpenTag device over Bluetooth LE, or upload a .zip of your own NTAG215 amiibo backups to the on-device library and emulate any of them from the OLED menu.

Device connection
Use Chrome or Edge on HTTPS / localhost.
Not connected
The device answers with status lines shown in the log below.
NDEF note

Plain text or HTTP(S) URL, up to 200 UTF-8 bytes. Written to NTAG215 tags or emulated as a virtual tag.

Amiibo library

Upload a .zip (or loose .bin files) of NTAG215 dumps from your own collection. Dumps are unzipped in your browser, validated, then copied to the device over BLE. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

On device

Use only backups of amiibo you own. Amiibo data is Nintendo proprietary; this page and the device never bundle or generate it — you supply your own dumps.

OpenTag is an independent open-source project. NFC writes replace the existing NDEF area of the selected NTAG215. Confirm on the device before writing; only use tags you own or are authorized to program.
